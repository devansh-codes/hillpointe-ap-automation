## What the AI did

1. Listed the package (41 files across 8 folders).
2. Converted the brief and AP policy `.docx` to markdown (pandoc) and read them in full.
3. Dumped every sheet of the 3 workbooks (master data, import template, ERP history) with openpyxl.
4. Extracted text from all 12 PO/contract PDFs and all 20 invoice PDFs (pdftotext `-layout`). Checked fonts and images to confirm every PDF has a text layer.
5. Noticed scattered letters in the 0017/0020 text, rendered those pages to PNG, and confirmed **REPRINT** and **PAST DUE** watermarks. That led to the decision to send PDFs to the model natively (text + image) instead of text-only.
6. Checked current Claude API capabilities (model IDs, PDF input, structured outputs) before choosing the extractor.
7. Worked through each invoice by hand against the policy: PO balances, pay-app math against history, COI dates, CO status, tax allocation, software allocation rounding, due dates. Wrote this up in `SOURCE_ANALYSIS.md`.
8. Compared 5 architectures on a weighted matrix and chose the hybrid (LLM extraction → deterministic rules). See `PLAN.md` and `DECISIONS.md`.
9. Created `governance/` and root `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md`.
